Planning a finishing farm?

The project owner commissions a review of the site, stock supply and buyer’s specification before committing to a finishing system.

  • Site layout, water source and existing equipment
  • Species, incoming sizes and seasonal availability
  • Buyer’s grade, volume and delivery window

You receive a written review of where supply and site conditions fit the buyer’s requirements, what is uncertain and whether a pilot is worth running.

Before you enquire

Practical
partnership questions.

What does the assessment fee cover?

The fee covers a written review of the options and evidence gaps, with a recommendation to proceed, revise the plan or stop. Equipment, installation and any field pilot require a separate proposal.

What happens if the evidence supports a pilot?

The site team and Crabionics agree one question to test, who will operate and pay for the trial, the records to keep and the basis for deciding whether to continue.

Is AquaOS available for my farm?

Crabee is the pond-management prototype within AquaOS. CrabDesk is a functional RAS grow-out prototype and demonstrates operator records with Moltie using sample data. Register interest to discuss a guided demonstration or a scoped pilot.

Where can I review the evidence?

The validation page describes the evidence for technical integration, biological performance and commercial fit, and what still needs field testing.
