What we prove before we scale

The project sponsor pays for the assessment. For a pilot, the site and its partners agree who covers each cost. Once a site can supply the agreed grade and volume reliably, buyers could purchase the stock they accept.

01

Scoped engagement

Will a customer pay to answer a specific production or monitoring question?

The first evidence is a customer paying for a written review with an agreed scope.

02

Defined pilot

Can site operators use the workflow on a normal shift?

Agree on a routine with the site team. Record when operators use it, what action they take and where they need help.

03

Proposed 600-box finishing validation

Can successive batches reach the buyer’s grade on time, at a measured cost?

Track stock entering the boxes, survival, harvest weight by grade and cost per kilogram that meets the buyer’s specification.

04

Repeatable cluster

Can a second site reproduce the result?

Compare grade, delivery timing, operator effort and cost across sites before adding more producers.
