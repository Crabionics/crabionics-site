Reusable patterns applied: problem-led entry points, producer jobs, practical system explanations, setting/question/measurement/next-gate validation language, direct expert contact, and truthful project-learning formats. Unsupported AI, autonomy, optimization, ROI, and commercial-outcome language was excluded from active public copy.
