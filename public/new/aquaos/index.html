Direct answers for farmers, finishing operators, technical partners and processors evaluating AquaOS.

What mud-crab farming software does Crabionics offer?

CrabDesk is our working RAS grow-out operator application, within the broader AquaOS platform. It brings intake, batches, individual habitats, sampling and manual water-test records into a workspace. The product film uses demonstration records.

Is CrabDesk working software or just a concept?

CrabDesk is working software. The public film shows captured application screens and Moltie responses with demo records. Access for external operators is arranged through an agreed pilot scope; the film is not evidence of commercial deployment or biological results.

Does AquaOS currently support pond farming?

Pond operations are an upcoming, separately developed workflow. Register your pond-production interest so the team can discuss your setting without representing the pond product as released.

Do I need sensors or an automated RAS system to use the software?

The demonstrated water-test records can be entered manually. CrabSense sensors, CrabPod equipment connections and closed-loop hardware control require separate integration and validation. No physical control capability is implied by this software demonstration.

What can I ask Moltie?

Moltie is the assistant visible in CrabDesk. The demonstration shows questions about sample operating records and supporting source links. Record-grounded accuracy, missing-data behavior and suitability for live use are assessed within the beta scope.

How do operators in Odisha or elsewhere request access?

Use the early-access form, confirm your email, and describe your region, farm type and operating routine. Crabionics reviews the setting and contacts suitable candidates about a guided demonstration or scoped pilot. Registration does not automatically grant an application account. For background on production records and preparing a pilot, explore our practical resources. We do not publish restricted SOPs, RAS setpoints or internal application access.
