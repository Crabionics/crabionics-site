02 / The water

Recirculate and treat the water.

Water from every habitat drains to collection tanks, passes through filtration, skimming and UV, and returns treated water. Treatment performance must be checked. BioPod / RAS supports water management where the setting requires it; treatment is sized to each site and its stock.

  2. Filter

03 / Observation

A reading needs context.

CrabSense connects environmental measurements to the right setting. Operators add biological observations and daily work. Measurement quality and maintenance matter as much as collecting data.

Explore the sensing role →
04 / Decision & response

Keep the action connected to its reason.

AquaOS links observations, decisions and operating history. Where integration is available, permitted commands pass to local equipment through CrabPod under operator oversight. Record the response, then observe what changed: an equipment acknowledgement and a biological outcome are different measurements.

Software and synthetic foundations exist. Physical integration and biological performance need their own accepted evidence. See what we are validating
