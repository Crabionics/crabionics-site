Conceptual schematic · integration in development
01 / The biology

Individual habitats for daily care.

One crab, one compartment. Teams can feed, check and handle each crab while reducing direct contact between animals. The design follows the crab’s size and production stage.

Individual care & observation Explore the habitat ↗
02 / The water

Recirculate and treat the water.

Water from every habitat drains to collection tanks, passes through filtration, skimming and UV, and returns treated water. Treatment performance must be checked. Treatment is sized to each site and its stock.

Circulation & treatment Follow the water system ↗
03 / The observation

Measure water conditions.

CrabSense is being developed to link each reading to the unit and stock it describes. Sensors need calibration and upkeep, so they work alongside manual checks.

In development Measurements in context Explore CrabSense ↗
04 / The response

Link operators with equipment.

CrabPod is being developed to connect local equipment with operator workflows. Command acknowledgement and independent state feedback are validation goals; completed integration is not implied.

In development Local equipment connection Explore CrabPod ↗
05 / The intelligence

Connect records. Support decisions.

AquaOS brings together Crabee for pond management and CrabDesk for RAS and crab-box operations, with shared Moltie assistance. These are functional software prototypes. Predictive capabilities and physical sensor-to-control integration require further development and validation.
